# VSM Female - 1.0.7 is out!

:exclamation: Remember to repair your mod :exclamation:

**What's fixed**
- **ACE Arsenal loadouts work again** - with ACE Arsenal Extended loaded, saved loadouts lost the female uniform and vest together with everything inside them. Ammo, grenades, medical, all gone
- **Straight boxes on the vests** - box magazine pouches, utility pouches and magazine shingles keep their shape now. On the female vests they were bent and crumpled, most visibly the belt boxes of the FAPC Gunner

**What's new**
- **The Gender switch stays** - still one arsenal entry per item, and the "Gender" option swaps between the original and the female version. The female items are regular arsenal items again, that is what keeps your loadouts intact
- **Respects limited arsenals** - if an arsenal only allows one of the two versions, the switch only offers that one

Your class names stay the same, loadouts you saved with 1.0.5 load correctly again.

All credit for the original gear goes to the VSM team: VSM, VanSchmoozin, Roadrunner, Spectre, Zeealex, Jarrad96, CrazyCorky, Bacon and Ghost :blue_heart:
