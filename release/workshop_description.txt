[b]VSM is the main mod. This is only an add-on for it.[/b]
Requires the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=823636749]VSM All-In-One Collection[/url] on the server and every client. All credit for the original gear goes to the VSM team.

VSM Female refits the VSM vests and uniforms to a female body: 206 vests and 361 uniforms, in every camo, with the same stats. The male items stay untouched. Female items are named like the original with (Female) at the end.

Optional: with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2522638637]ACE3 Arsenal Extended[/url], an item and its female version share one entry with a Male / Female switch.
