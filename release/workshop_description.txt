[b]VSM is the main mod. This is only an add-on for it.[/b] All credit for the original gear goes to the VSM team.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MissHeda/VSM-Female/main/.github/workshop/about.png[/img]
VSM Female refits the VSM vests and uniforms to a female body, in every camo and with the same stats. The male items stay untouched. Female items are named like the original with (Female) at the end.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MissHeda/VSM-Female/main/.github/workshop/features.png[/img]
[list]
[*][b]206 vests[/b] - LBT 6094, FAPC, RAV, carrier rigs, MBSS and more.
[*][b]361 uniforms[/b] - Crye G3, Battle Dress and the rest of the VSM range.
[*][b]Every camo, same stats[/b] - textures and armour come straight from VSM.
[*][b]ACE3 Arsenal Extended support[/b] - optional Male / Female switch on one entry.
[/list]
